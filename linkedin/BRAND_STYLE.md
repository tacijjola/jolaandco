# Jola & Co — Brand Style (memory / source of truth)

Use this for every graphic, post, and design so everything stays consistent.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Navy Slate** | `#2D3644` | Headlines, body text, icon circles, brand name |
| **Golden Wheat** | `#9F8551` | Accents, one highlighted word, thin rules, borders, tagline, CTAs |
| **Sage** | `#6B8F71` | Positive/"it works" accents, subtle highlights, hover states |
| **Sand Beige** | `#EDE6D6` | Alternating section backgrounds, soft panels |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Main background |

## Type
- **Font:** Poppins throughout (geometric sans).
- Headlines: Poppins **Bold**, large, tight leading; usually navy with **one word in gold**.
- Labels: Poppins Bold, **UPPERCASE**, letter-spaced.
- Body / sub-lines: Poppins Regular/Light, muted navy-grey.
- Tagline: Poppins, gold, UPPERCASE, wide letter-spacing.

## Layout conventions (the "house style")
- White background, generous whitespace, mobile-friendly.
- Thin **gold bar** across the very top.
- Section dividers: a thin gold horizontal rule with a small **gold diamond** in the centre.
- Icons: navy (or sage/gold) filled **circles** with simple white/gold glyphs; UPPERCASE label + 1–2 line description beneath.
- Vertical thin gold rules between columns.
- **Brand lockup footer (always):** `JOLA & CO.` in navy bold, then `AUTOMATE · ANALYSE · ADVANCE` in gold letter-spaced uppercase, with a small gold flourish beneath.

## Voice (captions)
- Warm, human, first-person, confident but not salesy.
- Short lines, lots of white space; reframing hook to open; `→` for bullets.
- Real edge to lean on: **20 years in operations** + now automation/BI/AI.
- End on an invitation or a question, not a hard pitch. Sign: `— Jola, Jola & Co.` + tagline. Occasional 🧡.
- Alternate **brand/philosophy** posts with concrete **value/how-to** posts.

## Tagline
**Automate. Analyse. Advance.**
