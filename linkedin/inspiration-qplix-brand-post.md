## Why this post works (what to steal)

- **Short, one-line sentences with lots of white space.** Reads effortlessly on a phone.
- **Values-led, not salesy.** It tells a belief, not a pitch.
- **Rhythmic triads:** "Different expertise. One responsibility. Helping clients decide."
- **Ends with an invitation to a conversation** — soft, human, no hard CTA.
- **Strong branded visual** with a tagline + a few clear pillars.

## How to adapt it for Jola & Co. (important)

The QPLIX post is a *company/hiring* post ("19 professionals, we're hiring"). Jola & Co. is a boutique/solo consultancy — so **keep the structure and tone, change the content.** Don't imply a team or hiring.

A Jola & Co. version could be a **philosophy post** about how you help founders:

- Hook, same shape: *"The smaller a business is, the more every wasted hour costs."*
- Belief: *"Growth shouldn't mean more admin. It should mean better decisions."*
- Triad: *"Clear data. Less busywork. Better decisions."*
- Pillars (reuse the ones from your infographic): **Automation · Business Intelligence · Operations** — or the four workflows (lead alerts, inbox, content recycling, reporting).
- Close with an invitation: *"If your team is drowning in repetitive work, I'd love to start a conversation."*
