## Main draft (my recommendation)

The smaller your business, the more every wasted hour costs.

A big company can absorb an afternoon lost to copying numbers between spreadsheets. A founder can't — it's *your* afternoon.

For twenty years I ran operations: the coordinating, the reporting, the quiet admin that keeps a business standing. I know exactly where the hours go.

Now I build the automations that give them back.

Not more software to learn.
Not another dashboard nobody opens.
Just the repetitive work — done, quietly, in the background.

Clear data. Less busywork. Better decisions.

That's the whole job.

If your week keeps disappearing into tasks a machine should be doing, I'd love to start a conversation. 🧡

— Jola, Jola & Co.
Automate. Analyse. Advance.

#Automation #Operations #Founders #Productivity #Make

## Why this one works
- **Leads with your real edge:** 20 years in operations. No other "I just learned automation" person can say that — it's your unfair advantage.
- **Values-led, not salesy.** Short lines, white space, easy on mobile (the QPLIX shape).
- **Triad:** "Clear data. Less busywork. Better decisions."
- **Ends on an invitation,** not a hard pitch.

## A note on the mix (for future posts)
Post 1 = credential. Post 2 = philosophy. Going forward, alternate these **brand** posts with concrete **value** posts — e.g. "3 automations any small business can set up this week," or a 30-second breakdown of one real workflow. Value/how-to posts tend to reach further and show potential clients what you can actually do — which is what turns into gigs.
